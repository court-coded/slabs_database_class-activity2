# SuperMart SA - Systems Analyst Report 

### Information System Mapping


**1. TPS (Operational)
-Checkout: Sales scan, payment (cash,card ,loyalty), receipt, real-time stock deduction, returns.
-Warehouse: Goods received Notes (GRN) , stock movement to shop floor , adjustments for damaged/expired stock, purchase orders.


**2. MIS AND DSS (Tactical)**
-MIS: Store management uses MIS for weekly sales performance. Reports: sales by dept , actual vs target,best-sellers vs slow-movers.
-DSS: When sales drop, DSS runs what-if analysis e.g "20% discounton weekends?" It analysis history, inventory, trends and forecasts profit to decide promotion.


**3. ESS (Strategic - CEO)**
-Reports: Annual profit/loss , total revenue, market share.
-Competitor Data: Competitor prices, new store openings.
-Forecasts: 5-year growth, expansion, online shopping impact.

**4. KWS and OAS**
-KWS: CAD for store layout design , power BI for analysis.
-OAS: Outlook for emails/calender , Excel/Word for reports, teams for meetings.

### 3. IT Landscape and legacy 

**Why legacy + cloud is complex:**
old system lack modern APIs, use different data formats , have securitygaps and no documentation, need middleware to connect.

** Cost saving with IT Landscape Diagram:**
1. EOL Tracking:Identifies unsupported apps still paying licenses.Retire early to save repair and license costs.
2. Standardisation: Shows duplication e.g 5 POS systems.Use 1 standard system to cut license , training and support costs.
